# Reservations

## 1. What reservations in Plate cover

Reservations in Plate manage the full flow from a guest request to a completed visit.

They include:

* Online booking requests and instant bookings
* Manual bookings for phone calls and walk‑ins
* Table assignments and section preferences
* Deposits, card guarantees, and payment status
* Status changes (pending, confirmed, arrived, seated, dining, payment, completed, no‑show)

## 2. Types of reservations

* **Online requests**
  * Guests book through the plate app, online widget or shared link.
* **Manual reservations**
  * Staff create bookings for:
    * Phone calls
    * Email enquiries
    * Walk‑ins who need a future time
* **Event or group bookings**
  * Larger parties with specific times, sections, or conditions.
  * Can be combined with deposits or card guarantees.

## 3. Reservation statuses you will see

Reservations move through clear stages:

* **Pending** – waiting for staff to accept or decline.
* **Confirmed** – accepted and scheduled.
* **Pending payment** – waiting for a deposit or card guarantee payment.
* **Arrived** – guest has arrived but may not be seated yet.
* **Seated / Dining** – guest is at the table and in service (seated, ordered, courses, payment).
* **Completed** – guest has left; visit is finished.
* **Cancelled by guest / restaurant** – cancelled with reason tracking.
* **Declined / auto‑declined** – request not accepted.
* **No‑show** – guest never arrived; updates their history.

These statuses appear consistently in the dashboard, bookings page, and analytics.

## 4. Creating a new reservation (staff)

You can create reservations from both the **Dashboard** and the **Bookings** page.

**Step‑by‑step**

1. Click **Add booking** or **New booking**.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (2).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

1. Enter:
   * Date and time
   * Party size
   * Guest name
   * Email and phone (when available)
2. Add any:
   * Special requests
   * Occasions (birthday, anniversary, etc.)
   * Preferred section or table type.
3. Choose:
   * Booking status (for example, confirmed vs pending)..
4. Save the booking.

The reservation will immediately appear in:

* Today’s dashboard
* The bookings list and calendar

## 5. Handling incoming booking requests

When your restaurant uses request‑based bookings:

1. New requests appear as **Pending** in:
   * The dashboard alerts
   * The bookings list
2. **Click** on a pending request to see:
   * Guest details and contact information
   * Requested time and party size
   * Any notes or special occasions
3. Choose to:
   * **Accept**&#x20;
   * **Decline** (with a note that can be shared)

Once accepted, the status changes to **Confirmed** and the booking flows into your normal service views.
