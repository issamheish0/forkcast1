# Settings

## 1. What you control in Settings

The **Settings** area lets you control how Plate behaves for your restaurant.

From here you can:

* Keep your public restaurant information up to date
* Set booking rules and operating hours
* Configure deposits and card guarantees
* Choose cuisine types, dietary options, and amenities
* Manage links for your online menu and booking widget

## 2. General restaurant information

In the **General** section you can manage:

* Restaurant name and description
* Physical address
* Phone and WhatsApp numbers
* Website URL
* Instagram handle

**Suggested steps**

1. Open **Settings → General**.
2. Confirm that the name and address match what guests will see on your website and profiles.
3. Add phone, WhatsApp, website, and Instagram so guests can reach you easily.
4. Save your changes.

## 3. Operating hours and availability

Plate uses your hours and availability to control when bookings are allowed.

In the **Operating Hours** area you can:

* Set opening and closing times by day of week.
* Add special days (holidays, events, or seasonal schedules).
* Adjust for special periods such as Ramadan using dedicated settings (where configured).

**Typical setup**

1. Go to **Settings →  Operating Hours**.
2. You have four tabs here\
   1\. Booking hours: When client can book\
   2\. Open hours: When you restaurant is open\
   3\. Special Hours: extended hours for a specific date or period\
   4\. Closures: Holidays and exceptional closures.

This ensures the widget and internal tools only allow bookings when you are actually open.

## 4. Booking rules

Booking rules control how far ahead guests can book and how cancellations are treated.

In the **Operation** section you can set:

* **Booking window** – how many days in advance guests can make a reservation.
* **Cancellation window** – how many hours before the booking guests are allowed to cancel without penalty.
* **Table Turnover** – how long a table is reserved for each booking (for example, 90 or 120 minutes).

**To adjust booking rules**

1. Open **Settings → Operation.**
2. Review the booking window, cancellation window, and dining duration.
3. Save your changes.

These rules affect what guests see and how bookings appear in your dashboard.

## 5. Deposits (core feature)

Deposits are configured in the **Deposits** settings and are available to all restaurants on Plate.

You can:

* Turn deposit requirements on or off according to your needs.
* Define which bookings should require a deposit (for example, certain party sizes or key dates).
* Configure deposit amounts and default service fee percentages where applicable.

**How deposits work with settings**

1. In **Settings → Deposits**, review your default rules:
   * When a deposit is required
   * How much is charged
   * Any fees added by your payment providers
2. Once saved, new bookings that match those rules will be marked as requiring a deposit.
3. Staff can then generate payment links directly from the booking.

For the step‑by‑step booking flow using deposits, see **Core Features → Reservations**.

## 6. Restaurant features and amenities

In **Features / Restaurant Profile** you can describe what your restaurant offers, helping guests make better decisions and powering internal rules.

You can set:

* **Cuisine type** and secondary cuisines.
* **Price range** (for example, budget, moderate, upscale, fine dining).
* **Dietary options** you support (for example, vegetarian, vegan, gluten‑free).
* Amenities such as:
  * Parking
  * Valet service
  * Outdoor seating
  * Shisha availability



This information is used in:

* Your public profile
* Internal filters and guest expectations

## 8. Public links (menu and booking widget)

Plate provides ready‑to‑use links you can add to your website, Instagram, or messages:

* **Menu link**
  * Shows your menu to guests.
* **Booking widget link**
  * Lets guests make bookings directly into Plate.

You can find these in **Settings → Links**.

To use them:

1. Copy the link from the Settings screen.
2. Paste it into:
   * Your website “Book now” button
   * Social profiles
   * Guest messages and emails
