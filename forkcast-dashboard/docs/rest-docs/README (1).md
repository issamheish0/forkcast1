# Getting started with Plate

## 1. Who should use this guide

This guide is for restaurant owners, managers, and host staff who are setting up Plate for the first time or rolling it out to a new team.

You do not need any technical background to follow these steps.

## 2. Accessing your account

1. **Open Plate in your browser or tablet:** [**www.plate-app.co/login**](https://www.plate-app.co/login)
2. **Sign in with your staff account.**
3. If you cannot sign in, contact your restaurant owner or manager so they can add you as staff inside Plate.

Once signed in, you will be taken to the main dashboard.

If you have multiple restaurants, you will be redirected to a page with a real-time overview of all your restaurants.

## 3. Check your restaurant profile

Before you start taking reservations, confirm that your basic restaurant information is correct.

1. Open the **Settings** section from the sidebar [www.plate-app.co/settings](https://www.plate-app.co/settings)
2. In the **General** tab, review:
   * Restaurant name
   * Description
   * Address
   * Phone and WhatsApp numbers
   * Website and Instagram (if you use them)
3. Update any incorrect details and save.

## 4. Configure basic booking rules

Plate uses a few key rules to control how guests can book.

In **Settings → Bookings**:

1. Set your **booking window** (how far in advance guests can book).
2. Set your **cancellation window** (how many hours before the booking guests can cancel without penalty).
3. Set your **standard dining duration** (how long a table is usually held for a booking).
4. Set your desired **age restriction** if any (This will prevent bookings for people under the minimum age set)

These rules control what guests see and how requests appear to your team.

## 5. Review operating hours and availability

To make sure Plate only accepts bookings when you are actually open:

1. Go to **Settings → Operating Hours.**
2. In the booking hours tab, you can set the date and time to accept online bookings and reservations.
3. In the open hours tab, it has nothing to do with booking. In this tab, you set the date and time where your restaurant is open, not to confuse with when your restaurant takes booking. IE: **You can be opened till 11:30pm but stop taking booking at 9:30pm.**
4. Add any special closures (public holidays, private events, Ramadan hours, etc.).
5. Save your changes.

## 6. Try a test booking

It is helpful for the team to see the full flow end‑to‑end.

1.  From the **Dashboard** or **Bookings** page, open the **New booking** or **Add booking** button on the bottom right of the screen.

    <figure><img src=".gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-03-12 at 12.44.42.png" alt="" width="107"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
2. Create a booking for today with:
   * Guest name and contact details
   * Date and time
   * Party size
   * Any notes or occasions
3. Confirm the booking.
4. Watch how it appears in the **Dashboard queue** for today

## 7. Training your team

When you introduce Plate to your staff:

1. Walk them through the **Dashboard**, **Reservations**, and **Settings** screens.
2. Practice:
   * Creating a booking
   * Checking in and seating guests
   * Handling a no‑show
   * Cancelling a booking

You can always return to this documentation to refresh how a feature works or to explore new areas of the platform as your team gets more comfortable with the basics.
