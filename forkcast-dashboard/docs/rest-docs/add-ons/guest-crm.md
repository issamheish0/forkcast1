# Guest CRM (Add‑on)

> **Note:** This feature is available as an add‑on. If you do not see the full Customers / Guest CRM tools in your dashboard (tags, VIP lists, imports, insights), it may not be enabled for your restaurant.

## 1. What the Guest CRM add‑on does

The Guest CRM add‑on turns your basic customer list into a full guest relationship system.

With Guest CRM you can:

* Tag guests (for example “VIP”, “Influencer”, “Family”, “Corporate”)
* Track visit counts, spend, and no‑show history more easily
* Highlight VIP and blacklisted guests in your day‑to‑day views
* Merge duplicate guest records
* Import guests from other systems
* Export your guest list for loyalty or marketing
* See suggested VIP candidates and other insights

## 2. How to know if Guest CRM is enabled

You likely have Guest CRM if:

* The **Customers** section shows:
  * Tag chips
  * Bulk actions (add tags, VIP, blacklist)
  * Import options
  * An “Insights” tab
* Customer detail panels include:
  * Tag controls
  * Detailed visit statistics
  * VIP / blacklist controls

If you only see a simple list with basic info and no tags or insights, you are probably using the core customer features without the add‑on.

## 3. Tags and segments

Tags help your team quickly understand what type of guest you are dealing with.

Examples:

* VIP
* Regular
* Corporate
* Influencer
* Family
* High‑spend

**Adding and managing tags**

1. Open **Customers → Tags** or the tag management tools in the Customers area.
2. Create tags with:
   * Name (for example “Local regular”)
   * Optional description (internal use only)
   * Color (to make it easy to recognize at a glance)
3. Save your tags.

**Assigning tags to guests**

1. In the **Customers** list, select one or more guests.
2. Use **Bulk actions** or the guest’s detail panel.
3. Choose which tags to apply or remove.

Tags appear:

* In the Customers table
* Inside booking details
* In the check‑in queue and dashboard (as small indicators next to guest names)

## 4. VIP management

VIP tools help you recognize and treat your most valuable guests consistently.

With Guest CRM you can:

* Mark guests as VIPs from:
  * The Customers list (tap on the three dots at the end of a customer row)
  * Add New customer
  * A customer’s detail panel



## 5. Blacklist and risk management

The Guest CRM add‑on also helps you track problematic guests.

You can:

* Mark guests as blacklisted.
* Record a reason (for example, repeated no‑shows, abusive behavior).
* See blacklist warnings when:
  * New bookings are created
  * Requests arrive from matching details
  * Staff open the guest’s profile.

**Blacklisting a guest**

1. Tap on the three dots at the end of a customer row.
2. Choose **Add to blacklist**.
3. Enter a short reason that your team will understand.
4. Save.

From then on, staff will see clear visual warnings before confirming new bookings for that guest.

## 6. Imports, exports, and merging duplicates

Guest CRM includes tools to keep your data clean and complete.

### Importing guests

1. Go to the **Customers** area.
2. Choose **Import guests**.
3. Download the sample file to see the required format.
4. Fill in your guest list (names, emails, phones, etc.).
5. Upload the file and review any mapping steps.
6. Confirm the import.

Imported guests will then appear in:

* The Customers list
* Future bookings (when matched by email or phone)

### Exporting guests

1. In the **Customers** area, choose **Export**.
2. Plate will generate a CSV file containing:
   * Basic contact info
   * Booking counts
   * Tags
   * Basic visit dates
3. Use this file for loyalty programs, newsletters, or external analysis.

### Merging duplicate guests

Over time, you may collect duplicate records (for example, same person with different emails).

Guest CRM helps you merge them:

1. Use the duplicate tools or insights in the Customers section to find possible duplicates.
2. Review suggested pairs, checking:
   * Names
   * Emails and phones
   * Visit history
3. Choose a primary record and merge secondary records into it.
4. Confirm to combine history and keep your data clean.

## 7. Customer insights

The Guest CRM add‑on includes an **Insights** view that can highlight:

* Suggested VIP candidates (based on visits and spend)
* Guests approaching visit milestones (for example, 10th or 25th visit)
* Guests with high no‑show rates
* Trends in party size, visit frequency, and more

These insights help you:

* Decide who to invite to special events
* Identify guests who might appreciate loyalty rewards
* Spot patterns in cancellations and no‑shows

## 8. How Guest CRM affects daily operations

With Guest CRM enabled, your staff will see more context in day‑to‑day screens:

* VIP and blacklist badges next to guest names
* Icons indicating important notes or dietary flags
* Tag counts on bookings (for example, “Corporate” or “Family”)
* Suggested guests to prioritize in follow‑up or marketing

This allows your team to:

* Recognize important guests as they arrive
* Make better seating and service decisions
* Keep a cleaner and more accurate guest database over time
