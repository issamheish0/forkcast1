## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. Access and accounts

**Q: I cannot log in to Plate. What should I do?**

- Make sure you are using the correct email address.
- Check with your owner or manager that you have been added as staff.
- If you still cannot log in, ask your manager to remove and re‑add your access from the staff tools.

**Q: How do I switch between restaurants if I work at more than one location?**

- If your account is linked to multiple restaurants, you will see a selector in the dashboard or sidebar.
- Choose the correct restaurant before you start taking bookings or seating guests.

---

### 2. Reservations and waitlist

**Q: A guest says they booked, but I cannot find their reservation.**

Try the following:

1. Search by:
   - Guest name
   - Phone number
   - Email
   - Confirmation code (if they have it)
2. Check both **today** and nearby dates in the **Bookings** page.
3. If you still cannot find it, treat them as a walk‑in:
   - Create a new booking
   - Add a note explaining what happened.

**Q: How do I handle no‑shows?**

1. Open the booking from the **Bookings** page or **Dashboard**.
2. Change the status to **No‑show**.
3. If the booking had a deposit or card guarantee, follow your policy for:
   - Keeping the deposit
   - Charging any no‑show fee

This will update the guest’s history and analytics.

**Q: Can I move a booking to another time or day?**

- Yes. Open the booking and edit:
  - Date
  - Time
  - Party size (if needed)
- Plate will re‑check availability. Resolve any warnings before saving.

---

### 3. Tables and floor plan

**Q: How do I change the table assigned to a booking?**

1. Find the booking in the **Dashboard** or **Bookings** list.
2. Use the floor plan or table tools to:
   - Select a new table
   - Confirm the change
3. Plate will warn you if:
   - The new table is too small
   - Another booking is already using that table at the same time.

**Q: A table shows as occupied, but the guests have left.**

1. Select the table on the **Dashboard** floor plan.
2. Open the related booking.
3. Change the status to **Completed**.

If this happens often, remind staff to complete bookings as part of their close‑out routine.

---

### 4. Deposits, guarantees, and payments

**Q: When should I ask for a deposit vs a card guarantee?**

- Use **deposits** when you want to collect money before the visit (for example, New Year’s Eve, special menus).
- Use **card guarantees** when you only want to charge a fee if the guest does not show up or cancels too late.

Your rules can be configured in **Settings**, and bookings will tell you which one applies.

**Q: How do I send a payment link to a guest?**

1. Open the booking.
2. If a deposit or guarantee is required, use the payment link action.
3. Copy the link or send it via:
   - WhatsApp (where supported)
   - SMS
   - Email
4. Confirm that the booking status updates once the payment is completed.

**Q: A guest paid the deposit but wants to cancel. What now?**

- Follow your restaurant’s policy:
  - If you are refunding:
    - Use your payment provider tools to refund fully or partially.
    - Update the booking notes.
  - If keeping all or part of the deposit, record this in the booking notes so your team is aligned.

### 5. Customers and Guest CRM

**Q: What is the difference between basic Customers and the Guest CRM add‑on?**

- **Basic Customers (core)** include:
  - Guest name, contact info, visit counts, and simple history.
- **Guest CRM (add‑on)** adds:
  - Tags, VIPs, blacklist, imports, exports, and advanced insights.

If you do not see tags or VIP tools, the Guest CRM add‑on may not be enabled for your restaurant.

**Q: How do I mark someone as VIP?**

1. Go to **Customers**.
2. Open the guest.
3. Use the **VIP** control to mark them as VIP.

They will then appear as VIP in the dashboard and reservations views.

**Q: How do I block a problem guest from booking again?**

1. Open the guest in **Customers**.
2. Add them to the **Blacklist** and write a short reason.
3. Future bookings using the same details will show a clear warning to staff.

### 6. Settings and configuration

**Q: Who can change booking rules and payment settings?**

- Usually only owners or managers with higher‑level access can change:
  - Booking window
  - Cancellation rules
  - Deposit and guarantee settings
  - Public links

If you cannot see or change these settings, contact your owner or system administrator.

**Q: How do I change our opening hours in Plate?**

1. Go to **Settings → Availability / Operating Hours**.
2. Adjust the hours for each day.
3. Save.

Changes will affect both the online widget and internal tools.

---

### 7. Devices, tablets, and notifications

**Q: Can I use Plate on multiple tablets at once?**

Yes. You can:

- Sign in on multiple devices with staff accounts.
- See live updates across all devices (subject to your internet connection).

**Q: I am not receiving push notifications.**

Check:

1. Notifications are allowed for Plate in your browser or device settings.
2. The device has been registered in the **PWA / Notifications** section.
3. Your internet connection is stable.

If problems continue, try:

- Logging out and back in
- Re‑installing the PWA on the device

---

### 8. Add‑ons and plan questions

**Q: I see references to features I do not have (for example, Guest CRM). Why?**

- Some parts of this documentation describe add‑ons that are not included in every plan.
- Each add‑on page starts with a note explaining that it is optional.
- If you are interested in enabling an add‑on, contact your Plate representative.

**Q: How do I know which add‑ons we have?**

- Look at your navigation:
  - If you see tags, VIP tools, and insights under **Customers**, Guest CRM is enabled.
  - If not, you are using the core customer features only.
- Your owner or manager can also confirm your current plan with Plate.
