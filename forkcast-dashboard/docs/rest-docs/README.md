# Welcome to Plate

## 1. Overview

Plate is a restaurant operations platform that helps you manage the full guest journey in one place. From online bookings and walk‑ins, to table assignments, deposits, card guarantees, guest history, and performance insights, Plate gives your team a live view of what is happening in the restaurant right now and what is coming next.

Plate is designed for use on tablets at the host stand, in the dining room, and in the office, so owners, managers, hosts, and floor staff can all work from the same live source of truth.

## 2. What restaurants can do with Plate

* **Manage reservations and walk‑ins**
  * Receive and review online booking requests from your website or booking widget.
  * Accept or decline requests with automatic conflict checks and suggested alternatives.
  * Create bookings manually for phone calls and walk‑ins, including shared tables and walk‑in “quick seat” flows.
  * Track booking status from request, confirmed, arrived, seated, in‑service, payment, to completed or no‑show.
* **Run the floor in real time**
  * View a live floor plan showing which tables are free, reserved, in use, or shared between parties.
  * See who is arriving soon, who is waiting to be seated, who is currently dining, and which tables need attention.
  * Move parties between tables and sections while Plate checks capacity and warns about conflicts.
* **Use a structured waitlist**
  * Capture walk‑ins on a waitlist with party size, preferred time, and contact details.
  * See how many parties are waiting, have been notified, or have been converted into bookings.
  * Convert a waitlist entry into a full booking when a table becomes available.
* **Collect deposits and card guarantees**
  * Require deposits for bookings when needed (for example, busy days or large parties).
  * Secure bookings with card guarantees and clearly defined no‑show and late‑cancellation fees.
  * Generate payment links for supported providers and send them to guests, including via WhatsApp.
  * Track whether a deposit or guarantee has been paid, refunded, forfeited, or charged.
* **Build rich guest profiles**
  * Maintain a guest list with visit history, total bookings, spend, and last visit.
  * Record dietary requirements, allergies, favourite cuisines, and preferred table types or time slots.
  * Tag guests (for example “VIP”, “Influencer”, “Family”, “Corporate”) for quick recognition.
  * Store internal notes about preferences, behaviour, and special occasions.
  * Mark guests as VIPs or add them to a blacklist with reasons.
* **See performance and analytics**
  * View booking, revenue, occupancy, and service‑time trends over days, weeks, or months.
  * Analyze which hours are busiest, how quickly tables turn, and how full your restaurant runs.
  * Track menu and category performance, average order values, and profit estimates.
  * Monitor staff activity, order processing times, and service quality indicators.
  * Review customer ratings and review trends over time.
* **Control restaurant settings and profile**
  * Configure your public restaurant profile (name, description, address, contact details, website, Instagram).
  * Set booking rules such as booking window, cancellation window, and standard dining duration.
  * Configure deposit and card‑guarantee rules, including fees and where they apply.
  * Define cuisine types, dietary options you can accommodate, and amenities like parking or outdoor seating.
  * Manage operating hours, availability, and special calendars (events, holidays, Ramadan, etc.).
  * Copy ready‑to‑use links for your menu and booking widget to embed into your website or share with guests.
* **Use Plate as a tablet app**
  * Install Plate as a Progressive Web App (PWA) on tablets and phones.
  * Receive push notifications about new requests and important booking changes.
  * Continue working with cached data when the connection is unstable.

## 3. How the system works

Plate is built around the real‑time flow of guests through your restaurant:

1. **Guest discovers and books**
   * Guests can book using your online booking widget or menu link, choosing date, time, party size, and adding notes such as occasions or dietary needs.
   * Depending on your settings, bookings may be confirmed instantly or arrive as “requests” that staff must review.
2. **Request review and confirmation**
   * New booking requests appear on the dashboard and in the bookings area with clear indicators for requested time, party size, and notes.
   * Plate checks for clashes with existing bookings and highlights potential conflicts.
   * Staff can accept, decline, or suggest alternatives; when accepted, bookings move to “confirmed” and can optionally be assigned tables.
3. **Deposits and guarantees (when required)**
   * For bookings that require a deposit or card guarantee, staff can generate payment links directly from the booking.
   * The system calculates fees and total amounts, and provides links that can be copied, opened, or sent to the guest (including via WhatsApp).
   * Booking status clearly shows when payment is still pending and when it has been successfully completed.
4. **Pre‑service planning**
   * On the day, the main dashboard shows confirmed bookings for today, arrival windows, and overall occupancy.
   * The unified floor plan displays tables by section, capacity, and assigned bookings, including shared‑table setups.
   * Staff can move bookings between tables and sections, or prepare sections based on expected demand.
5. **Arrival, check‑in, and seating**
   * As guests arrive, staff can check them in from the check‑in queue or directly from the floor plan.
   * If a table is already assigned, Plate verifies that it is still suitable; if not, staff can select a different table with capacity checks and warnings.
   * Walk‑ins can either be added to the waitlist or seated immediately using the quick‑seat flow, which creates a booking and assigns a table in one step.
6. **During service**
   * Table status updates as guests move through the meal: arrived, seated, ordered, courses in progress, and payment.
   * The floor plan and booking lists show who is currently dining, how long they have been at the table, and how many guests are still expected.
   * Staff can quickly update statuses, switch tables, add internal notes, and see VIP or blacklist flags before interacting with the guest.
7. **Payment and completion**
   * When payment is in progress, the booking status reflects this, including cases where a deposit or guarantee is involved.
   * Staff can mark bookings as completed when guests leave, or mark them as no‑show if they never arrived, with automatic updates to guest history and statistics.
8. **After the visit**
   * Customer profiles are updated with visit counts, no‑show history, loyalty points (where used), spend estimates, and notes.
   * Analytics pages summarize performance, showing which days and hours perform best, how full the restaurant runs, and how service times are trending.

## 4. Main areas of the platform

* **Dashboard**
  * Live view of today’s operations with real‑time stats.
  * Unified floor plan showing table status by section, including shared tables.
  * Check‑in queue and waitlist side panel for managing arrivals, walk‑ins, and quick‑seat actions.
  * Critical alerts highlighting pending requests, unassigned confirmed bookings, and tables that may cause conflicts.
* **Bookings**
  * Full list, calendar, and table‑view of bookings across days and weeks.
  * Filters by status (pending, confirmed, dining, completed, cancelled, no‑show) and by time of day.
  * Manual booking tools for phone reservations and walk‑ins, including confirmation codes and table assignments.
  * Service progress overview showing how long parties have been dining and how much time remains based on their expected duration.
* **Waitlist**
  * Dedicated page for managing current and historical waitlist entries.
  * Stats for active, notified, converted, expired, cancelled, and today’s new waitlist entries.
  * Tools to notify guests, update status, and convert a waitlist entry into a confirmed booking.
* **Customers**
  * Guest CRM with search, filters, and bulk actions.
  * Customer profiles with visit history, spend, tags, VIP/blacklist status, dietary info, and internal notes.
  * Tools for importing guests, merging duplicates, managing tags, and exporting the customer list to CSV.
  * Insights on VIP candidates, loyalty patterns, and visit milestones.
* **Analytics**
  * Overview dashboard with bookings, revenue estimates, table usage, staff performance, and rating summaries.
  * Dedicated views for revenue, bookings, customer behaviour, menu performance, and operational metrics such as turnover and wait times.
  * Visual charts for hourly patterns, daily trends, category performance, and cost estimates.
* **Settings**
  * General restaurant details: name, description, address, phone, WhatsApp, website, and social links.
  * Restaurant features: cuisine, price range, dietary options, parking, outdoor seating, shisha, and age restrictions.
  * Operating hours and availability, including special days and events.
  * Deposits and card‑guarantee rules and configuration.
  * Links for your public menu and booking widget, plus PWA and push notification setup.
* **Menu, Orders, and Staff (where enabled)**
  * Menu management for items and categories, including basic analytics on top items and categories.
  * Order tracking for in‑house orders and kitchen workflows, feeding into analytics for preparation times and order volumes.
  * Staff management features such as shifts, roles, and access‑controlled areas of the dashboard.

## 6. Who this guide is for

This guide is written for:

* **Restaurant owners** who want a clear view of how Plate supports operations and growth.
* **General managers and floor managers** who run daily service, staff, and performance.
* **Host and reservations staff** who handle bookings, arrivals, seating, and guest communication.
* **Supervisors and senior servers** who need visibility into table status, waitlists, and high‑value guests.

Technical implementation details are intentionally excluded; you do not need any technical background to use Plate effectively.

## 5. How to use this documentation

This introduction is the starting point for understanding what Plate can do for your restaurant. The rest of the documentation dives into each area of the platform in detail, with step‑by‑step guides and examples for tasks such as:

* Handling new booking requests and managing the waitlist.
* Using the floor plan to assign, move, and manage tables.
* Creating and managing deposits and card guarantees.
* Working with guest profiles, tags, notes, VIPs, and blacklists.
* Reading analytics to understand bookings, revenue patterns, and service performance.
* Configuring your restaurant settings, booking rules, availability, and public links.

You can read the sections in order or jump directly to the feature you want to use today. Each section is written for busy restaurant teams and focuses on practical actions you can take inside Plate.
